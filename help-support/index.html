SILQ Help & Support

High-fidelity mockups for the Help & Support module. Browse Help and My Tickets live as a full page in the left sidebar. The floating FAB is for quick ticket submission from any page.

SILQ Shell with Help FAB

The SILQ app shell showing an RFQ Detail page with the floating Help button bottom-right, status banner, and the Help & Support nav item in the sidebar.

Help & Support — Browse Help

Full-page Help & Support section (via sidebar nav). Browse Help tab with search, contextual article cards, popular topics, and all articles.

Help & Support — Article View

KB article opened full-page inside Help & Support — "Understanding Quote TAT" with body content, info callout, and helpfulness feedback.

FAB → Report Issue — Bug Filled

Pre-filled bug report: description, "Bug" issue type, severity, functional area, related record, expected behavior, steps to reproduce.

Screen 6

Screenshot Annotator

Full-bleed dark overlay with the captured SILQ page, annotation toolbar (arrow, rectangle, highlight, blur, text), color swatches, and attach button.

Screen 7

Help & Support — My Tickets

Full-page My Tickets tab with filter chips, search, and a table of tickets with status badges, severity, category, and "New reply" indicators.

Screen 8

Help & Support — Ticket Detail

Full-page ticket detail with two-column layout: activity timeline with reply composer on the left, status pipeline and details sidebar on the right.
