Understanding Quote TAT

5 min read · Updated 3 days ago

Quote Turnaround Time (TAT) is the time between when an RFQ is submitted and when the corresponding quote is generated and made available. Understanding how TAT is calculated helps set accurate expectations with your customers.

How TAT is calculated

SILQ measures TAT from the moment an RFQ transitions to "Submitted" status. The clock starts ticking and stops when the first quote is generated, regardless of whether it's an auto-quote or a manual quote from a carrier.

TAT calculation diagram

Factors that affect TAT

  • Trade lane popularity: High-traffic lanes (e.g., Shanghai → Rotterdam) typically have faster auto-quote availability.
  • Container type & volume: Standard FCL 20/40 containers are quoted faster than specialized equipment (reefer, flat rack).
  • Carrier response time: For manual quotes, TAT depends on carrier response speed. SILQ sends reminders after 24h.
  • Validity period requests: Longer validity periods may require additional carrier approval, increasing TAT.
Tip: You can view real-time TAT benchmarks for your most-used trade lanes under Reports → Quote Analytics. This helps you set accurate expectations with your customers.

If a quote hasn't been generated within the expected TAT window, check the RFQ status page for any validation errors. Common issues include missing port codes, invalid commodity codes, or weight/volume mismatches.
