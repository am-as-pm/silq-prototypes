Stories 1 and 2

How work moves

Read top to bottom. One card sits in one lane at a time. Colored chips are the lanes on the board.

1
Off board Shipper bookings

Booking arrives

New shipper booking lands in Shipper bookings. It is not an RFQ yet.

  • Convert: becomes an RFQ. Usually Draft first. Can skip Draft and land in Submitted.
  • Cancel: leaves the board. Never quoted.
  • Card click: existing Bookings page.
2
Draft

Optional Draft

AM is still filling the RFQ. Not yet in quoting.

  • Submit: moves to Submitted.
  • Delete: leaves the board. Nothing is quoted.
3
Submitted

Quote it

Ready to price. Stays here until a quote is sent.

  • Open card: existing Review Quotes page.
  • Send quote: moves to Awaiting Client.
4
Awaiting Client

Wait for the client

Quote is out. Only three things can happen next.

Accepted

A. Client accepts

  • First-time accept: stays in Accepted until the AM sends the booking email, then leaves the board.
  • Requote accept: leaves the board immediately. No stay in Accepted.
Rejected

B. Client declines

  • Lands in Rejected.
  • New quote: back to Submitted.
  • Cancel: off the board. No loop.
Expired

C. Validity lapses

  • Lands in Expired (requote pending).
  • Reissue: back to Submitted.
  • Cancel: off the board. No loop.

Story 5 · CON-7896

Summary strip

This is the daily starting point. Pulse tells what changed. Do next tells what to do.

Pulse

  • One line: new RFQs and new bookings since yesterday (PT).
  • Pending to quote = Submitted only, plus how many already waited a day.
  • New rejections.
  • Cleanup only if too many CRD-stale cards.
  • Never a Do next row.

Do next

Show three rows, in this order. A row focuses that card.

  • Quote now (Submitted waiting)
  • Convert now (booking waiting)
  • Send booking
  • Follow up
  • Reissue
  • Win back

See all

  • Opens the full ranked list.
  • Closes Astra.
  • Pick a row to focus that card.

Astra

  • Opens chat about this board.
  • Closes See all.
  • Does not move cards or change the list.

Story 3 · CON-7880

Filters

All of these stay on this page. They stack: a card must match every active control.

Who and what

  • Assignee: one person. AM or associate on this pod.
  • Organization: one or many clients.
  • Service: FCL, LCL, Air, Inspections. Default: all.

Find

  • Sort: inside each lane. Recent / earliest submitted, or CRD up / down. Bookings use booking date where that applies.
  • Search: RFQ name, Silq id, booking name, booking id.

Chips

  • High priority: only flagged high-priority cards.
  • Needs attention: only cards the AM starred.
  • Requotes: only requote cards.
  • CRD passed: cargo-ready date at least 2 days past.

Defaults (off at first)

  • Include demo: demo cards stay hidden until this is on.
  • Include Air: on the Expired lane only. Expired Air stays hidden until this is on.
