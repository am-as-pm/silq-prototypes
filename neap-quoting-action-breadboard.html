How work moves

One card sits in one lane at a time. Follow the orange arrows for the user journey.

User journey

Solid arrows are the usual path. Dashed arrows skip Draft, loop a requote, or leave on requote accept.

Lane notes

Same journeys, with the AM actions on each lane.

1
Off board Shipper bookings

Booking arrives

New shipper booking lands in Shipper bookings. It is not an RFQ yet.

  • Convert: becomes an RFQ. Usually Draft first. Can skip Draft and land in Submitted.
  • Cancel: leaves the board. Never quoted.
  • Card click: existing Bookings page.
2
Draft

Optional Draft

AM is still filling the RFQ. Not yet in quoting.

  • Submit: moves to Submitted.
  • Delete: leaves the board. Nothing is quoted.
  • Card click: existing Review Quotes page (same as other RFQ lanes).
3
Submitted

Quote it

Ready to price. Stays here until a quote is sent.

  • Open card: existing Review Quotes page.
  • Send quote: moves to Awaiting Client.
4
Awaiting Client

Wait for the client

Quote is out. Client accept and decline are the clean exits. Validity is not a third exit from this lane.

Accepted

A. Client accepts

  • First-time accept: stays in Accepted until booking email is sent, then leaves.
  • Requote accept: leaves immediately. Never sits in Accepted.
  • If a first-time Accepted quote lapses before the booking email: moves to Expired.
Rejected

B. Client declines

  • Lands in Rejected. Stays until a new quote path starts.
  • New quote: back to Submitted.
  • Cancel: off the board.
Awaiting Client Expired

C. Validity lapses

  • Still Awaiting: stays in Awaiting. Do not move to Expired.
  • Expired lane: latest client-facing quote is expired and the RFQ is back at Submitted, or first-time Accepted lapsed before booking email. A requote that left the board can re-enter here if it later expires.
  • Reissue: back to Submitted.

Story 5 · CON-7896

Summary strip

This is the daily starting point. Pulse tells what changed. Do next tells what to do. A row focuses the card on the board. It does not open Review Quotes.

Pulse

  • One line: new RFQs and new bookings since yesterday (PT).
  • Pending to quote = Submitted only, plus how many already waited a day.
  • New rejections, or “no new rejections”.
  • Cleanup only if more than 6 CRD-stale cards.
  • Never a Do next row.

Do next

Show three rows, in this order.

  • Quote now: Submitted waiting ≥ 1 day
  • Convert now: booking waiting ≥ 1 day
  • Send booking: Accepted ≥ 1 day, first-time only
  • Follow up: Awaiting at risk, silent, or Needs attention
  • Reissue: Expired lane (Air never included here)
  • Win back: Rejected

View All

  • Opens the full ranked list, grouped by verb.
  • Closes Astra.
  • Pick a row to focus that card.

Astra

  • Opens chat about this board.
  • Closes View All.
  • Does not move cards or change the list.

Story 3 · CON-7880 · Include Air in CON-7878

Filters

All of these stay on this page.

Who and what

  • Assignee: one person. AM or associate on this pod.
  • Organization: one or many clients.
  • Services: FCL, LCL, SCL, Air, Inspections. Default: all. A card with two modes shows if either mode is selected.

Find

  • Sort: inside each lane. Default: earliest submitted. Also recently submitted, or CRD up / down.
  • Search: booking id, booking name, RFQ name, RFQ number (Silq id).

Chips

  • High priority / Needs attention / Requotes / CRD passed: turning one on clears Assignee, Organization, and Services.
  • Requotes: RFQ requotes only. Bookings are out.
  • CRD passed: cargo-ready date at least 2 days past.
  • Include demo: off at first. ANDs with everything else. Does not clear the other filters.

Expired lane only

  • Include Air: off at first, resets each visit. Expired Air stays hidden until this is on. Does not change the summary strip.
