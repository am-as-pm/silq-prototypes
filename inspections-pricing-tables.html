Inspections pricing

Two tables the quote will read later. Upload a CSV, or add a row. Download what is on screen. A row stays valid until a newer upload for the same key replaces it.

Product & Category mapping 0

Units one inspector can cover in a man day. These checks come from the column headers.

  • Product is one product, or ALL for the whole category.
  • Units inspectable in a man day is a whole number greater than 0.
  • Valid from is the uploaded-on date. The row stays in force until a newer upload for the same category and product replaces it.
  • Uploaded by is required. Dates in a file are DD/MM/YYYY. A blank uploaded-on date is stamped with today.

Pricing matrix 0

Charge per man day and the margin already inside the sell. These checks come from the column headers.

  • Service level is Final, Inline, Pre production, Factory, TOP, or ALL.
  • Charge per man day is greater than 0. Margin is a percent, 0 or higher. 10 and 10% both mean 10 percent.
  • Total sell is calculated: charge × (1 + margin). A file total that does not match is rejected.
  • A newer upload for the same category, service level, and country replaces the older row. Dates in a file are DD/MM/YYYY. Uploaded on is the valid-from date.
